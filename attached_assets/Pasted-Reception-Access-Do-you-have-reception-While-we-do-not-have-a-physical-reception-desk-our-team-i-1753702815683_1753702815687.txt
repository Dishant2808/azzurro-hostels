Reception & Access
Do you have reception?
While we do not have a physical reception desk, our team is available onsite from 10 AM – 6 PM at all locations, with 24/7 virtual support via WhatsApp and the Goki app.

Do you use physical keys?
No, all rooms use digital keys via the Goki app for secure and convenient access.

What is the Goki confirmation code?
It links your reservation to the Goki app. You’ll receive it via email once your keys are created. If not, please contact us.

Check-In & Check-Out
What do I need to do for check-in?

Submit your details (DOB, email, phone, and a clear ID/passport photo) to frontdesk@azzurrohotels.com

Download the Goki app

Complete your payment before check-in

When can I check in?
Check-in is available between 3 PM – 10 PM. If you’re arriving after 10 PM, please let us know in advance.

Can I check in earlier than 3 PM?
Rooms are not ready before 3 PM. However, you're welcome to wait in the common area or store your luggage.

Can I check in after 10 PM?
Yes, late check-in is possible. Please be mindful of others in shared rooms. If you don’t check in on the reserved day, your key will expire.

What time is check-out?
Check-out is at 10 AM.

When will I receive my room info and keys?
Keys and room details will be sent via Goki/email/text by 3 PM on your check-in day.

I’m changing rooms. When will I get the details?
Room details will be shared by 10 AM via the Goki app or WhatsApp.

Luggage & Accessibility
Can someone help with my luggage?
Yes, maintenance staff may assist if available at your arrival time.

Can my friend help with my luggage?
Unfortunately, only guests are allowed in the rooms. No outside assistance is permitted.

Can I store my bags before check-in?
Yes, you can store luggage free of charge before 3 PM.

Can I store bags after check-out?
Yes, we offer free storage until 8 PM. After 12 PM, availability may be limited.

Do you have lifts?
No, our properties currently do not have elevators.

Can we be placed in the same room?
We can’t guarantee it, but we’ll do our best based on availability.

Do you have laundry machines?
Yes.

Surry Hills: $3 washer/dryer (coins)

Central: $3 washer/dryer (coins)

Pyrmont & Allen: $4 washer/dryer (card only – Visa/Mastercard)

Do you provide laundry detergent?
No, please bring your own.

Do you have towels and linens?

Linen: Provided

Towels: Available for $10 rental (held on card until returned)

Do I need my own pillow?
A pillow is provided, but you may bring your own if preferred.

Do you provide shampoo or soap?
We provide hand soap only. Please bring your own personal care items.

Do you have hair dryers?
No, we currently do not provide hair dryers.

Meals & Dining
What time is breakfast?
Breakfast is served daily from 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM.

What time is dinner?
Dinner times vary by property. Please contact our front desk via Goki or WhatsApp for details.

Nearby Facilities
Are there gyms or coffee shops nearby?
Yes! There are multiple options within walking distance. Ask our staff for recommendations.

Long-Term Stays
Do you offer long-term discounts?
Yes. Please contact us via Goki or WhatsApp for rates based on your stay duration.

Parking
Do you offer parking?
We have free street parking outside most properties. These are not reserved or secured, so availability is first-come, first-served.
